GT. architects' journey started back in 2020. Ar. Geo Thomas who is the founder and the principal of the firm graduated by securing 1st rank in bachelor of architecture from Mahatma Gandhi University, Kerala. He is also a Green Rating Integrated Habitat Assessment (GRIHA) accredited professional who strives to bring cohesive solutions to our built environment.

“ We at our studio aim to produce design solutions that are in rhythm with the site and its surroundings. We consider effective planning to be a thorough understanding of the inside in parallel with the outside. Our philosophy directs us to make conscious and deliberate decisions to trace out the built within its context by focusing on the common thread of end-user experience”

“We follow form-follows- site rule by the book. And services include undertaking all the macro to micro designing in detail, Designing should always be approached holistically at all levels including Architecture, Interior and Landscape”

“An intangible continuity to the realm of tangible senses"
